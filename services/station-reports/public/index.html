02 / REPORT IN THE APP

Spot a gap? Send it from CardBal.

The reporting screen is coming in a CardBal app update. Once it is available in your app:

  1. 1
    Open a saved card

    Use Station Data, or open a trip and choose “Report or Correct a Station.”

  2. 2
    Describe the station

    Add a name. Codes, lines, operator, city and other clues are welcome.

  3. 3
    Pick a location if useful

    Place a pin on the map or choose your current location. The location is optional.

Card ID hashes, app-specific device IDs, app versions, and private notes are stored for review but never shown on this website.
