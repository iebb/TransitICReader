CardBal 2.6.3 for macOS improves card details and Japanese IC card colours.
It is a universal Mac Catalyst app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, signed
with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

New with a USB reader:

- Kitaca, nimoca, TOICA, PiTaPa and icsca cards without a card face show in
  their own colour.
- Card details omit unreadable balances and fares.
- Singapore concession and other CEPAS cards can be read; balances are
  identified as the balance stored on the card.
- Japanese IC history omits undated and unrecognised rows.

CardBal requires macOS 13 Ventura or later and a compatible external
USB NFC / CCID reader.

Download `CardBal-2.6.3-macOS.zip`, open it, and move CardBal to Applications.
`SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the download checksum.
