# CardBal 2.6.4 for Mac

The iPhone App Clip adds a quick card balance check from a link. Mac card reading continues to use an external reader.

New with a USB reader:

- China T-Union cards show Wuhan Metro stations and more stations in Chengdu,
  Qingdao, Guangzhou, Ningbo, Foshan, Nanjing and other cities.
- Hangzhou City Union cards and Lingnan Pass cards in Guangzhou and Foshan show
  metro station names.
- Renamed stations are updated in Qingdao, Xi'an, Tianjin, Dongguan, Lanzhou
  and on Beijing BRT.

Requires macOS 13 or later and a compatible USB NFC / CCID reader.

Download `CardBal-2.6.4-macOS.zip`, open it, and move CardBal to Applications.
