CardBal 2.6.2 for macOS brings the Mac app up to date with CardBal 2.6.2 for
iPhone, including everything from 2.6.1. It is a universal Mac Catalyst app for
Apple silicon and Intel Macs, signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

Download `CardBal-2.6.2-macOS.zip`, open it, and move CardBal to Applications.
CardBal requires macOS 13 Ventura or later.
A compatible external USB NFC / CCID reader is required to scan cards on a Mac.

New with a USB reader:

- Japanese IC cards show their own name: Suica, PASMO, ICOCA, manaca, TOICA,
  PiTaPa, Kitaca, SUGOCA, nimoca, Hayakaken and icsca.
- Reads Hanica, the Hankyu Bus and Hanshin Bus card, with its recent trips.
- Reads Lingnan Pass and Yang Cheng Tong cards without the China T-Union mark.
- Cards that carry only City Union read again, as they did before 2.6.0.

Also new: Send Feedback in Settings, to report a problem or share an idea with
up to four screenshots, and automatic crash reports. Both carry only technical
details and aren't linked to you or used for tracking.

The repository and this tag include the application source, build instructions
and third-party code and data license notices. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains the
download checksum.
